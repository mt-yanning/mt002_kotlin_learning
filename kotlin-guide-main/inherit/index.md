# Inheritance & Polymorphism

In this section, we consider the idea that classes can inherit attributes
and behaviour from other classes, adding their own attributes and behaviour
as well as altering the behaviour they have inherited.

We look at [how to create these 'subclasses'][sub] in Kotlin. We also look
at the impact of [overriding inherited methods][over] in a subclass. This leads
us to an [extended case study][study], a graphics application that demonstrates
the concepts of dynamic binding and polymorphism.

We conclude the section by examining the idea of [multiple inheritance][mult]
and explaining why Kotlin doesn't support this feature.
